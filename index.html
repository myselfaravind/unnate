Case study · Concept work

UNNATE, built by UNNATE.

Our own identity and website — the clearest example of how we work, because every decision is ours to explain.

  1. Problem

    A new studio offering websites, creative, short-form video and campaigns risked reading as “just another web designer” — or as four unrelated services.

  2. Strategy

    Lead with one idea — people see your brand before they choose you — and present every service as part of the same first impression. Give the mascot a job: making the brand memorable.

  3. Execution

    The wordmark and orange character, a sky-and-cloud visual world, social creative in three directions, a motion reel, and this site with separate desktop and phone compositions.

  4. Result

    The site you’re reading — tested from 320px phones to 1920px desktops, keyboard accessible, and calmer for people who prefer reduced motion. We’ll add performance data once there is enough to report honestly.

How we work

Five stages, in this order.

Each stage answers one question before the next begins. Nothing gets designed until we know what it needs to say, and nothing gets built until the design is approved.

  1. 01

    Understand

    Who is this for, and what does the business need its digital presence to do?

    Covers business, audience, goals, your existing website and content, and your current digital presence.

  2. 02

    Strategy

    What should each piece say, and what should people do next?

    Covers positioning, messaging, page structure, content priorities, the customer journey, and the job each piece of communication has to do.

  3. 03

    Create

    How does it look, read and feel — everywhere it appears?

    Covers website experiences, social creative, short-form video and campaign assets, all built around the agreed direction.

  4. 04

    Launch

    Is it ready to put in front of real customers?

    Covers development, publishing, campaign setup, testing, and the practical steps required to put the work into use.

  5. 05

    Improve

    What is working, and what should change next?

    Covers reviewing what performs, iterating creative and campaigns, and improving your digital presence over time where relevant.

What that means when you work with us.

You see the reasoning

Every structural and design decision comes with the reason behind it, so you can question it before it’s built.

Everything is labelled honestly

Concepts are marked as concepts. We don’t show results we can’t verify.

Launch isn’t the finish line

The site is set up to be measured, so the next improvement is based on what visitors actually do.
