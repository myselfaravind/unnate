What this demo shows, and what a real build adds

This is the front end of a Workflow Tracker, running entirely in your browser with sample data. It shows the everyday screens: one list of jobs, statuses named the way the team talks, a board view, a job record with a checklist, notes and history, search, filters and a spreadsheet export.

A real build adds what a demo cannot: staff sign-in with admin and staff roles, a shared database so everyone sees the same jobs, daily backups, file attachments, and your current spreadsheet imported on day one.
