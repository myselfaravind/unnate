why we work this way

Small businesses tell the same stories about web projects.

The designer who stopped replying, the domain registered in someone else's name, the price that grew, the launch date that kept moving. Those problems are rarely about skill. They come from things nobody wrote down.

So we sell fixed packages with published prices, write every scope down before taking money, keep your domain and data in your name, and test our work before handing it over.
