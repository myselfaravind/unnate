What else will I pay for?

Costs paid to others

  • Your domainPaid by you to the registrar, in your name. Usually a small yearly fee.
  • HostingA static hosting account in your name. The free plan is usually enough for a small-business site.

How and when do I pay?

Payment terms

  • When50% when you approve the written scope; 50% after you approve the finished work, before launch.
  • HowBy bank transfer against an invoice. Monthly fees are invoiced monthly from launch; cancel with 30 days' notice.
  • CurrencyPrices are in US dollars. Ask if you would like to be invoiced in Australian dollars or pounds sterling.
  • TaxesPrices exclude any tax your country charges on services bought from overseas. Your accountant can tell you whether this applies to you.
  • If you cancelBefore work starts: full refund of the deposit. After work starts: the deposit covers the work done, and you keep that work.
