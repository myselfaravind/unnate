What a clinic website has to do

Most patients meet your clinic on a phone screen first.

Before anyone books, they want to know what you treat, who will treat them, where you are and how to get in touch. A good clinic website answers those questions quickly and calmly. These are the jobs we design every clinic website around.

  1. 01

    Be clear on a phone, in seconds.

    People often look up a clinic on their phone, in between other things. We design mobile-first: readable type, short pages and the next step always within reach.

  2. 02

    Explain treatments in plain language.

    Patients search for a problem, not a procedure name. Treatment pages say what it is, who it’s for and what to expect, in the words patients use, reviewed by your clinicians for accuracy.

  3. 03

    Make calling and booking effortless.

    Tap-to-call, an appointment request form, or a link to the booking system you already use, placed where people look for it, not buried in a footer.

  4. 04

    Show where you are and when you’re open.

    Address, map, opening hours, parking and landmark directions, on every page that needs them.

  5. 05

    Introduce the people and the place.

    Practitioners, qualifications and real photographs of your clinic build confidence before the first visit. We plan this content with you.

  6. 06

    Look as careful as your care.

    A calm, consistent look, fast pages and accessible design tell patients something about the standard of care they can expect.

What we deliver

Everything a clinic website needs, and nothing it doesn’t.

Every practice is different, so we agree the exact scope with you. Most clinic websites are built from these four parts.

01 The pages

Pages patients actually use.

  • A home page that says what you treat and where
  • Treatment or service pages
  • About the practice and your team
  • Contact, location and opening hours
  • Answers to common questions
  • Photographs of your clinic and facilities

02 The contact paths

Every route to your front desk.

  • Tap-to-call buttons on phones
  • An appointment request form, sent to your inbox
  • A WhatsApp chat link, if your clinic uses it
  • Map, directions and parking notes
  • Links to your Google Business Profile and reviews
  • Or a link to the booking system you already use

03 The foundations

Fast, accessible and easy to find.

  • Mobile-first, responsive design
  • Fast-loading pages and optimised images
  • Readable type, strong contrast and keyboard-friendly navigation
  • Page titles, descriptions and a sitemap for search engines
  • Visitor analytics, if you want them

04 Launch & care

Launched properly, then looked after.

  • Domain and hosting setup
  • Content entry and final checks on real phones and laptops
  • A walkthrough of how to update the site
  • Maintenance support after launch, as agreed

and help writing the words, if you want it

How it works

From the first call to launch, in five clear steps.

  1. 1

  2. 2

  3. 3

  4. 4

    We take it live, check every form and link, show you how to make updates, and stay on hand afterwards.

How we handle clinic content

Clear, honest and checked.

A healthcare website carries more responsibility than most. People arrive worried, comparing options, or in a hurry. So the content on every clinic site we make follows a few simple rules.

  1. 01

    Your clinicians have the final word.

    We help structure and write treatment pages. Anything clinical is reviewed and approved by your team before it goes live.

  2. 02

    No promises the care can’t keep.

    No guaranteed results and no exaggerated claims. Before-and-after images only with patient consent, and only if you want them.

  3. 03

    Real people, real rooms.

    We recommend photographing your team and your clinic. Where stock images are used, they never pretend to be your patients or your staff.

  4. 04

    Usable by everyone.

    Readable type, strong contrast, clearly labelled forms and keyboard-friendly navigation, so the site works for older patients and for anyone using assistive technology.

Patients should feel looked after before they arrive.

    Questions clinics ask

    Good to know.

    Something else on your mind? Ask it in the enquiry form and we’ll answer it properly.

    Do you only work with dental clinics?

    No. Maren is a dental demo, but the same approach works for general medical clinics, specialist practices, physiotherapy, skin and aesthetic clinics, spas and wellness businesses.

    We already have a website. Can you redesign it?

    Yes. We start by looking at what your current site does well and where patients get stuck, then recommend whether to refresh it or start again.

    Can patients book appointments online?

    We can add an appointment request form that sends the details to your inbox, or link to the booking or practice-management system you already use. Tell us how your front desk works and we’ll suggest the simplest option.

    Will the website help us appear on Google?

    We build every site with the foundations search engines look for: fast pages, clear headings, page titles and descriptions, a sitemap and a layout that works on phones. We can also help you connect it to your Google Business Profile.

    No one can honestly guarantee rankings or patient numbers, and we won’t.

    Who writes the content?

    We can. We talk to you, draft the pages in plain language and send them for review. Clinical information is always checked and approved by your team. If you prefer to write it yourselves, we’ll shape and edit it.

    How long does it take?

    It depends on the number of pages and how ready your content and photographs are. After the discovery call, we give you a timeline you can plan around.

    What does it cost?

    Every clinic needs something slightly different, so we quote once we understand the scope. You’ll have a clear written proposal before any work begins.

    Do you look after the site after launch?

    Yes. We can handle updates, maintenance and small improvements as agreed, or show your team how to make changes themselves.
