Before you buy

Website questions.

Can I edit the site myself?

Not through a drag-and-drop editor: we build fast static sites, which is why they load quickly and have nothing to update. Small edits are included in Website Care, or quoted per job. If you want to change your site every week yourself, a builder such as Squarespace or Wix may suit you better, and we will say so.

Where will my site be hosted?

On a static hosting service, in an account in your name. For a typical small-business site the free plan is enough, so there is usually no monthly hosting bill. You pay for your domain directly to the registrar.

Will it show up on Google?

We set up the basics: page titles and descriptions, local-business details, a sitemap, fast loading and a link to your Google Business Profile. We do not promise rankings; nobody honest can.

I already have a website. Can you redesign it?

Yes. That is what Five Pages is for. We redirect your old page addresses to the new ones so existing links and search results keep working.

Can you build an online shop?

For a small range of products, yes, on a hosted shop service in your name. Shops are not part of the fixed packages because the work depends on the number of products, payments and delivery options, so we quote after a call.

What happens after launch?

We fix any fault in what we built for 30 days at no cost. After that you can take Website Care at $29 a month, or ask for changes when you need them; they are quoted first.
