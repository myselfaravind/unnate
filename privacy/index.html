Privacy

Privacy notice

Draft for legal review. This notice has not yet been reviewed by a lawyer and is not yet in force. It describes how we intend to handle personal information.

Who we are

UNNATE is a small studio based in India that builds websites and custom business software. You can contact us about privacy at the email addresses at the bottom of this page.

What we collect

  • Enquiries. When you use the project form we receive your name, business name, email, country, what you need, your current website if you give it, and your message.
  • Clients. When you become a client we keep the contact details, project files, scope documents and invoices needed to do the work and keep our accounts.
  • Software you ask us to host. The information you and your team put into the app belongs to you. We handle it only to host, back up and support the app, on your instructions.

How this website works

  • This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and does not track you across other sites.
  • The project form is delivered to our email by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-forwarding service, which processes the form contents to send them to us.
  • The job tracker demo saves your test changes in your own browser's storage. Nothing you type in the demo is sent to us.

Why we use it

To reply to your enquiry, to prepare a quote, to deliver and support the work you buy, and to keep financial records. We do not sell your information and we do not add you to marketing emails.

Where it is handled

We work from India, so information you send us is accessed from India. Our email and hosting providers may store it in other countries. For clients in the UK, we put an international data transfer agreement in place before handling personal information on your behalf.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not become projects: up to 2 years. Client records: as long as the law requires for financial records. Hosted app data: until you ask us to delete it or 30 days after the end of hosting, after giving you a full export.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing us. If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to your local regulator, for example the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK or the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
