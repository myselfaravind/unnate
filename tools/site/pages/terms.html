Terms

Terms of business

Draft for legal review. These terms have not yet been reviewed by a lawyer and are not yet in force. Every project is governed by the written scope you approve, which will refer to the final version of these terms.

1. The scope

Before any payment we send a written scope listing what we will build, what is not included, the price, the number of change rounds and the delivery date. Work covered by the scope is done for the price in the scope.

2. Payment

Websites and software builds: 50% when you approve the scope, 50% after you approve the finished work and before launch. Monthly services are invoiced monthly from launch. Prices exclude any tax your country charges on services bought from overseas.

3. Content and delivery

The delivery date counts from the day we receive the content listed in the scope. If content arrives late, the date moves by the same number of working days.

4. Changes

Each package includes the change rounds stated in the scope. A round is one combined list of changes. Extra rounds and work outside the scope are quoted in writing before we do them.

5. Ownership

Websites: on full payment, you own the website we built for you, including its design, code and the words we wrote. Third-party items such as fonts, photos and tools stay under their own licences. Software: the scope states who owns the code; your data is always yours.

6. Your accounts

Your domain and website hosting are registered in your name. You give us access to make the changes you ask for, and you can remove that access at any time.

7. Hosting and support for software

Software we host requires a monthly hosting and support plan. It covers daily backups kept for 30 days, security updates, fixes, and the amount of small changes stated in the plan. Either side can end it with 30 days' notice; you then receive a full data export and 30 days of read-only access.

8. Fixes after launch

For 30 days after launch we fix, at no cost, anything we built that does not work as described in the scope.

9. What we do not promise

We do not guarantee search rankings, enquiries, sales or revenue. We are responsible for the work we deliver, up to the amount you paid for it.

10. Cancelling

If you cancel before work starts, we refund the deposit in full. If you cancel after work starts, the deposit covers the work done and you receive that work.

11. Confidentiality and data

We keep your business information confidential and use it only for your project. Where we handle personal information on your behalf, we act on your instructions and sign a data processing agreement on request.

12. Law

Which country's law governs these terms is still to be decided with legal advice.
