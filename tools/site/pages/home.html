Before you ask

Questions people ask first.

Ask us something else [[arrow]]
Why are your prices lower than local agencies?

We work from India, we are a small team with low overheads, and we build on a tested base we reuse for every client. The price covers a clearly defined scope; anything outside it is quoted before we do it.

Do you have client reviews?

Not yet. We are new to this offer. Instead of reviews you can click through our working demos and read our own published test report. Client work will appear here only with the client's written permission.

Who owns the website or software?

The domain, hosting account and your data are in your name from the start. Website code is yours on final payment. For software, you own the code written for you, with a permanent licence to the shared base we reuse. How ownership works.

What if I need changes after launch?

For 30 days after launch we fix anything we built that does not work as agreed, at no cost. After that, small website changes are covered by Website Care ($29 a month), and anything larger is quoted in writing first.

Can you guarantee more customers or Google rankings?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. We build the things that make enquiries easier: clear service pages, fast loading, a visible phone number, working forms and local-business details. Results depend on your market and your follow-up.

Where are you, and when can I reach you?

We are in India and work Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 19:00 India time. That is the afternoon and evening in eastern Australia and the morning in the UK. We reply to every enquiry within one working day.
