What else should I know?

Questions people ask first

Why are your prices lower than local agencies?

Our costs are in India, the packages are fixed so there is no open-ended work to price in, and our websites are fast static sites with no plugins or licences to pay for. The trade-off is scope: everything included is listed, and anything else is quoted before we do it.

Do you have client reviews?

Not yet for this offer. Instead of asking you to trust reviews, we let you check the work: click through the demos, read the test report for this site, and pay the second half only after you have approved the finished work.

Where are you, and when can I reach you?

We are in India and work Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 19:00 India time. That is the afternoon and evening in eastern Australia and the morning in the UK. We reply to every enquiry within one working day.

Who owns the website or software?

Your domain, hosting account and data are always in your name. On final payment you own the website we built for you. For software, see who owns what.

Can you guarantee more customers or Google rankings?

No, and be wary of anyone who does. We build the site properly: fast, clear, mobile-ready, with search basics set up. What it brings in depends on your market and your work.

How do I pay?

By bank transfer, against an invoice. 50% to start and 50% after you approve the finished work. Software hosting and support is billed monthly from launch.
