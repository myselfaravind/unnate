About

A small studio that writes things down.

UNNATE is run by Aravind and Saran from India. We build websites and small business software for service businesses in Australia and the UK.

Why we work this way

Small businesses tell the same stories about web projects: the designer who stopped replying, the domain registered in someone else's name, the price that grew, the launch date that kept moving. Those problems are rarely about skill. They come from things nobody wrote down.

So we sell fixed packages with published prices, write every scope down before taking money, keep your domain and data in your name, and test our work before handing it over.

What we are honest about

  • We are in India, not in your city. We work Australian afternoons and UK mornings, and reply within one working day.
  • We are new to this offer and have no client reviews for it yet. Our demos and our test report are there so you can judge the work yourself.
  • We do two things: websites and custom business software. We do not sell SEO retainers, ads or online shops.
  • If ready-made software would serve you better than custom work, we will tell you.
