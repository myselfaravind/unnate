Seven steps

From first message to launch, and after.

  1. 1

    Enquiry and a 30-minute call

    Free · reply within one working day

    Send the form or an email. We book a short video call, or answer by email if you prefer. We say which option fits, or that you do not need us.

  2. 2

    Written scope and fixed price

    Within 2 working days · you approve

    One page: what we build, what is not included, the price, the change rounds and the delivery date. Work starts when you approve it and pay 50%.

  3. 3

    Content

    The delivery clock starts here

    You send the words, photos and details we listed, or for written packages we hold the content call. The date counts from the day we have everything.

  4. 4

    Design, build and review

    Private preview link · 2 change rounds

    You see the work on a private link and send one combined list of changes per round. Software gets a test version with your real data imported.

  5. 5

    Testing

    The report is yours to keep

    Phone, tablet and desktop; every link, form and button; page speed, accessibility checks and search basics. You get the results.

  6. 6

    Sign-off and launch

    You approve · balance paid · we launch

    We launch on your domain and hand over every login and file. Software users get a short guide and a walkthrough call.

  7. 7

    After launch

    30 days of fixes included

    Anything we built that does not work as agreed, we fix free for 30 days. After that, Website Care or software support covers you, or we quote changes as you need them.

Straight answers

What happens, in plain words.

  • How do you understand what I need?A free call about how your business works today and what is not working, followed by a written scope you can correct before you approve it. For bigger software, a paid scoping week.
  • How are designs reviewed?On a private preview link you can open on your own phone. You send one combined list of changes per round.
  • How do revisions work?Two rounds of changes are included (three for an Operations App). Extra website rounds are $39 each; software changes beyond the scope are quoted first.
  • How is the project delivered?Launched on your domain, with a test report, every login, and for software a short guide and a walkthrough call.
  • Who owns the result?Domain, hosting account and data are in your name from the start. Website code is yours on final payment. For software you own the code written for you, with a permanent licence to the shared base.
  • What support is there after launch?30 days of fixes at no cost. Then Website Care ($29 a month) or software hosting and support ($49 or $99 a month).
  • What if I need changes later?Small ones are covered by your monthly plan. Anything larger is quoted in writing before we start, and never added to your invoice afterwards.

Ownership and hosting

Yours, in your name.

  • Domain

    Registered in your name, on your account. We never register a client's domain in ours.

  • Website ownership

    On final payment you own the website we built: its design, code and the words we wrote. Photos and fonts keep their own licences.

  • Website hosting

    A static hosting account in your name, with us added as a user so we can make the changes you ask for.

  • Software hosting

    Hosted by us, because backups and security updates need an owner. Your data can be exported at any time.
