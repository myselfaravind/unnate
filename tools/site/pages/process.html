How we work

No surprises, because it is all written down.

The same steps for every website and every piece of software, with two points where nothing moves until you approve.

  1. 1. Enquiry and a 30-minute call

    Free · reply within one working day

    Send the form or an email. We book a short video call, or answer by email if you prefer. You tell us what the business does and what is not working; we say which package fits, or that you do not need us.

  2. 2. Written scope and fixed price

    Within 2 working days of the call · you approve

    A one-page scope: what we build, what is not included, the price, the change rounds and the delivery date. Work starts when you approve it and pay 50%.

  3. 3. Content

    The delivery clock starts here

    You send the words, photos and details we listed, or for written packages we hold the content call. The delivery date counts from the day we have everything.

  4. 4. Build and review

    Private preview link · 2 change rounds

    You see the work on a private link and send one combined list of changes per round. Software gets a test version with your real data imported.

  5. 5. Testing

    The report is yours to keep

    We test on phone, tablet and desktop: every link, form and button, page speed, accessibility checks, and search basics. You get the results.

  6. 6. Sign-off and launch

    You approve · balance paid · we launch

    Once you approve and pay the balance, we launch on your domain and hand over every login and file. Software users get a short guide and a walkthrough call.

  7. 7. After launch

    30 days of fixes included

    Anything we built that does not work as agreed, we fix free for 30 days. After that, Website Care or software support covers you, or we quote changes as you need them.

What are the ground rules?

Ownership, hosting and support

  • DomainRegistered in your name, on your account. We never register a client's domain in ours.
  • Website ownershipOn final payment, you own the website we built: its design, code and the words we wrote for you. Photos and fonts keep their own licences.
  • Website hostingA static hosting account in your name, with us added as a user so we can make changes you ask for.
  • Software hostingHosted by us, because backups and security updates need an owner. Your data can be exported at any time.
  • SupportEmail support for every client. Website Care ($29/month) and software support ($49 or $99/month) include a set amount of small changes.
  • Changes outside the scopeQuoted in writing before we do them. Never added to your invoice afterwards.

How do we talk?

Communication

  • HoursMonday to Friday, 10:00 to 19:00 India time. That is the afternoon and evening in eastern Australia, and the morning in the UK.
  • RepliesWithin one working day, by email. Calls are booked in advance.
  • UpdatesA short written update at every stage, with the preview link.
  • One contactEach project has one named person at UNNATE who answers your messages.
