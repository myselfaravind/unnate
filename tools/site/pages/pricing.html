What changes the final price

The things that make a project bigger.

  • Pages and content

    More pages, or asking us to write the words, moves a website up a package.

  • Features

    Online booking, a shop, more than one language or a blog you edit yourself.

  • Workflows and users

    For software: how many kinds of work it tracks, and whether customers or partners log in.

  • Connections and old data

    Linking accounting, calendars or email services, and moving data from an old system.

  • Rounds of changes

    Two are included (three for an Operations App). More are $39 each on websites, quoted on software.

  • Deadlines

    We quote real dates. If you need it sooner, we say whether that is possible before you pay.
