The forest's last lamp went out an hour ago. You are carrying what's left of it.Mothlight

You are a moth the size of a thumbnail, and the dark has started asking questions.

Mothlight is a three-hour walk through a forest that has forgotten what light is for. Fen & Fathom drew every tree by hand, twice, because the first set looked too friendly. There are no enemies. There are things that would simply prefer you put the lamp out.

  • One button. Hold to rise, let go to fall.
  • Light is your map. You only ever see as far as you can glow.
  • Fireflies are the story. Each one you catch remembers something.
  • No text after the title screen. The forest does the talking.

Built because the idea wouldn't leave.

Every cart on the shelf was made by a team you could seat around one kitchen table. There was no roadmap and no season pass. Somebody had an idea that kept them up, and they built it until it was done.

Oddcart collects those games and keeps them playable. That is the whole job.

  • Small teams

    The biggest studio here is four people and a dog called Patch. Patch has a credit.

  • Odd ideas first

    If a game can be fully explained in a meeting, it probably belongs on someone else's shelf.

  • Finished, not endless

    Most carts end in an afternoon. A few stay with you for years anyway.

  • Personal on purpose

    You can tell who made each one. Their handwriting is in the pixels.

  • Rules are optional

    One cart here is an elevator. One is a bus stop. Nobody asked permission.
