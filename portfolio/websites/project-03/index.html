Modern dentistry,a calmer visit.

A tooth is built in layers, and so is trust. Here is what you can expect at every appointment, from the first scan to the long view.

  1. Enamel, the surface

    See everything we see.

    Intraoral scans and photographs are reviewed together, on a screen at chair height, before any treatment is suggested. You leave with a copy of your plan.

  2. Dentin, the structure

    Time built into every appointment.

    Our appointments are scheduled with room to spare, so questions are answered properly and nothing about your care feels rushed.

  3. Pulp, the sensitive core

    Comfort, taken seriously.

    We explain each step before it happens, agree a signal for pausing, and talk through local anaesthetic and sedation options that may suit you.

  4. Root, the foundation

    Care for the long run.

    We favour keeping your natural teeth wherever that's realistic, prevention over repair, and written costs agreed before any course of treatment begins.
