Custom or off the shelf?

Often, ready-made is the better buy. Here is how to tell.

Ready-made job software such as ServiceM8, Tradify or Jobber is good value for standard trade work, and some plans cost less than $50 a month. We would rather say so than sell you something you do not need.

Custom fits when

  • Your work does not follow the quote, schedule, invoice pattern those tools are built around.
  • You use one part of a big product and pay for all of it, often per person.
  • Customers, landlords or partners need to see or send requests.
  • You want the data in your own app, exportable whenever you like.

Off the shelf fits when

  • You are a sole trader or a crew of two doing standard jobs.
  • You need card payments, GPS tracking or an app-store app now.
  • You want something running this week.
  • A $29–$50 monthly plan already does what you need.

Who owns it, and is it safe?

Your data, your rules.

Your data

Always yours. Export it as a spreadsheet at any time. If you leave, you get a full export and 30 days of read-only access.

The code

Operations App and Custom System: you own the code written for you, with a permanent licence to the shared base. Workflow Tracker: you use it while hosted with us, with an option to buy a handover copy.

Hosting and support

Daily backups kept 30 days, security updates, fixes, and up to 30 minutes (Tracker) or 1 hour (Operations App) of small changes a month.

Security basics

Encrypted connections only, secure sign-in, permissions checked on the server, and a log of who changed what.

What we do not store

Card numbers or health records. If your workflow needs either, we point you to a specialist product.

UK clients

Because we support the app from India, we sign a data processing agreement and the UK's international data transfer agreement before launch.

Before you decide

Software questions.

Is this a subscription product?

No. It is software built for your business. The monthly fee pays for hosting, backups, security updates and support, not for renting a product other companies also use.

Does it work on phones?

Yes, in the phone's web browser, and you can add it to the home screen. It is not an app-store app, and it needs a connection to work.

Can it connect to Xero, QuickBooks or my calendar?

An Operations App includes one connection where the other service allows it. A Workflow Tracker exports a spreadsheet you can import into most accounting tools.

What if we need changes later?

Small changes are included in the monthly support time. Bigger changes, such as a new workflow or a new kind of user, are quoted in writing before we start.

How do you measure whether it helped?

Before we build, we write down three numbers with you, such as status-check calls a day, time to put the weekly job list together, and jobs overdue. We check the same numbers 30 days after launch. We do not promise savings in advance.

What if I stop paying for hosting?

You get a full export of your data and 30 days of read-only access, so nothing is lost.
